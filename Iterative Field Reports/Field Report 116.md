## 0. The budget question, answered before the build

Asked what attack vectors fit a 30-day GPU workload, the honest answer turned
out to be **that 30 days is the wrong budget — too large, not too small.**

Measured throughput (FR98 rates, 32 cores at 0.6 scaling, GPU estimated at 15×):

```
CPU 32-core LGM      30.8 M/s  ->  30 days = 8.0e13 seeds
GPU 5080 (est)      462   M/s  ->  30 days = 1.2e15 seeds
```

| vector | cost | verdict |
|---|---:|---|
| **Double Fisher-Yates, full range** | 1.7 × 10¹⁰ | **~25 min on 32 cores** |
| Other PRNG families, 20 × 2³² | 8.6 × 10¹⁰ | ~45 min |
| Shuffle + post-rotate | 1.8 × 10¹¹ | ~1.6 h |
| Two-stage, derived second seed | 2.2 × 10¹² | ~19 h |
| Shuffle + post-affine | 1.5 × 10¹³ | ~5 d |

| Two-stage, **independent** seeds (2⁶²) | 4.6 × 10¹⁸ | **3,850× the 30-day GPU budget** |
| Three-stage (2⁹³) | 9.9 × 10²⁷ | 10¹² × |
| Arbitrary `C` (83!) | 4 × 10¹²⁴ | hopeless |

> **There is a gap and nothing sits in it.** Everything reachable costs under
> 2 × 10¹³; the next tier is nearly four thousand times a full month. Buying 30
> days instead of 7 changes nothing.

## 1. CHALLENGE I — what "double back-to-back" means

Three readings, and only one is both cheap and motivated:

- **(a) one seed, one continuing RNG stream, two consecutive FY passes.** The
  second pass consumes the draws following the first. Space 2³¹ / 2³². *This is
  what writing the shuffle call twice produces, which is the documented habit.*
- (b) two independent seeds — space 2⁶², out of reach per §0.
- (c) shuffle, re-seed from the result, shuffle again — needs a seeding rule
  there is no evidence for.

**(a) implemented.** Direction is a free choice per pass, so all four LGM
combinations are covered, plus both fastrand mappings.

**The gap is real:** FR99 exhausted **single** shuffles over the full spaces;
doctrine records the double variant swept only to **2 million seeds**.

## 4. Honest pricing of this vector

**Prior: low.** FR99 exhausted his *actual* generators — verified from his
source, both mappings, both directions, both orientations, full ranges. Every
remaining sweep vector is a variant of *"he did something other than what his
code does."* FR101 then found no 83-element asset anywhere in the game data,
removing the last construction family with a mechanism behind it.

**The arbitrary-`C` reading remains the most likely explanation, and no amount
of compute touches it.**

This vector is worth running because it costs twenty minutes and closes a
genuinely unswept region that his documented idiom points at — not because it
is likely to hit.
