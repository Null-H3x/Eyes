## 3. The honest accounting of EYESPIRAL-C

Adopted at FR119 because 118 cycles had produced no artifact. Seventeen cycles
later:

**Standing:** the first valid relative decryption; the cost-ranked repair space;
repair C found by systematic search; bijectivity⟺no-T1 proven by exhaustion;
the external verifier; the corpus viewer; the hypothesis catalog.

**Withdrawn:** six major claims.

**Verdict, and it should be recorded rather than spun:** the constructive turn
produced real artifacts and an equal number of retractions, at a much higher
rate of both. Every retraction was caught by the gates — the system worked —
but *a cycle that ships and then withdraws is not obviously better than one
that never ships.* The genuine gain is that the project now has a correct
reading and a mapped repair space, neither of which existed at FR118. The
genuine cost is that six intermediate artifacts were wrong while they stood.
